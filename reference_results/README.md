# Reference numerical outputs

These small CSV files preserve selected outputs of the study analyses. They support a quick inspection of the reported calculations and the compact plots without retraining. They are not substitutes for the official JMP raw workbooks and were not newly regenerated during packaging.

* `E04_component_cv_*`: all models in the four country-holdout settings; `*_mean`/`*_std` are across-repetition summaries. Quality-ratio errors are on the 0–1 scale; safely managed (`sm`) errors are percentage points.
* `E07_masking_summary.csv`: estimator bias, RMSE, MAE and 90th-percentile absolute error across the 200 masking replicates, in percentage points. The regional-mean result is retained alongside the ML results.
* `E08_series_bounds_m49l1.csv`: matched population-weighted aggregation and available-criterion dependence calculations, 2000–2024. `gap_people_M` is a population-equivalent accounting quantity in millions; `pop_bn` is population in billions.
* `E11_*`: country-preserving completion and dependence summaries. `pop_t` is the JMP population in thousands. `sm_lo`/`sm_hi` and `consistent_ml_lo90`/`consistent_ml_hi90` are fifth/ninety-fifth model-draw percentiles. Country intervals cannot be independently aggregated to recreate the global interval because the model includes shared regional draws.
* `E12_gap_by_group.csv`: accounting differences by country grouping. The country-level decomposition is regenerated by the supplied code.
* `E14_measurement_curves.csv`: global model-interval widths in percentage points under measurement-priority rules. Random lower/upper curves are the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles across 200 random priority orders.
* `E09_*`: threshold-model and linear-extrapolation diagnostics, including weak results.
* `vintage/E15_*`: retrospective country/area validation summaries. `as published then` uses earlier-edition non-quality inputs; the updated-input setting uses the 2025 edition. `lacking_*_M` is in millions; coverage and width metrics are empirical diagnostic summaries.
* `vintage/E16_*`: global edition replication and matched-year revisions.

All country names and codes are reporting-unit labels from JMP; the tables contain no household microdata or personal records. Display formatting belongs to the plotting code; these tables retain the original numeric values.
